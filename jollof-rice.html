Jollof Rice

West Africa · Nigeria

About this dish

Jollof rice is a one-pot rice dish cooked in a sauce of blended tomatoes, red peppers and scotch bonnet. It is served at weddings, birthdays and family gatherings across West Africa, and Nigeria, Ghana and Senegal each have their own proud version. Many food historians trace the dish back to the Wolof people of the Senegambia region, and the friendly “jollof wars” between countries show how much it means to people.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 ripe tomatoes, blended
  • 2 red bell peppers, blended
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper (adjust to taste)
  • 2 onions (one blended, one sliced)
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 0.5 cup vegetable oil
  • 3 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp salt

Cooking instructions

Click a step to open or close it.

Step 1: Prepare the sauce
  1. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnet and one onion until smooth.
  2. Pour the mixture into a pot and boil it until most of the water has gone and the sauce is thick.
Step 2: Cook the base
  1. Heat the oil in a large pot and fry the sliced onion until soft.
  2. Add the tomato paste and fry for 5 minutes, then pour in the thick sauce.
  3. Add the thyme, curry powder, bay leaves and salt and cook for 10 minutes.
Step 3: Cook the rice
  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then add it to the pot.
  2. Pour in the hot stock, stir once, cover tightly and cook on low heat for 25 to 30 minutes.
  3. Fluff with a fork and serve hot with plantain, chicken or salad.
