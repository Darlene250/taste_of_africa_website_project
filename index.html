Latest food trends

Ideas shaping how people cook and enjoy African food today.

Teff is getting famous

The ancient grain teff, used for injera, is gluten-free and rich in nutrients, so more cooks around the world are trying it in breads, pancakes and porridge.

Street food goes global

From grilled suya skewers to spiced street snacks, African street food is inspiring food trucks and pop-up kitchens, with bold pepper and spice blends at the centre.

Plant-forward cooking

Dishes built on beans, leafy greens and cassava leaves, like Rwandan isombe, show how African traditions already offer delicious plant-based meals.
