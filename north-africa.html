North Africa

Slow-cooked stews, warm spices and fragrant breads.

Food culture

North African food mixes Mediterranean, Arab and Amazigh (Berber) traditions. Wheat is the heart of many meals, in the form of couscous and bread, while olive oil, dates and preserved lemons add flavour. Warm spices such as cumin, saffron and cinnamon perfume the stews.

Staples

  • Wheat and couscous
  • Olives and olive oil
  • Dates
  • Chickpeas and lentils
  • Lamb and chicken

Key flavours

  • Cumin
  • Saffron
  • Cinnamon
  • Paprika
  • Ras el hanout

Cooking techniques

  • Slow cooking in a clay tagine pot, which keeps the stew moist.
  • Steaming couscous several times so the grains become light and fluffy.
  • Preserving lemons in salt to give dishes a deep, tangy flavour.
  • Baking flatbreads in communal or home ovens.
