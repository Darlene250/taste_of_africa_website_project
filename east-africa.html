East Africa

Fermented grains, hearty stews and shared plates.

Food culture

East African cooking ranges from the fermented flatbreads of the Ethiopian highlands to the maize porridge served across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda. Teff, maize, beans, plantains and leafy greens are common staples, and meals are often shared from a single big plate.

Staples

  • Teff
  • Maize (ugali, ubugali)
  • Sorghum
  • Plantain
  • Beans
  • Cassava leaves

Key flavours

  • Berbere
  • Cardamom
  • Garlic
  • Ginger

Cooking techniques

  • Fermenting teff batter for days to give injera its gentle sour taste.
  • Cooking injera on a large flat griddle called a mitad.
  • Stirring flour into hot water until it forms a firm porridge such as ugali.
  • Simmering greens and stews slowly, often with palm oil or groundnuts.
