Isombe

East Africa · Rwanda

About this dish

Isombe is a much-loved Rwandan dish made from pounded cassava leaves simmered slowly with eggplant, onion and garlic, and often finished with palm oil. It is traditionally served with ugali (ubugali), rice, plantains or sweet potatoes, and is a familiar dish at family tables and celebrations in Rwanda and nearby countries.

Ingredients

  • 500 g pounded cassava leaves (frozen is fine)
  • 1 eggplant, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 cup spinach, chopped (optional)
  • 3 tbsp palm oil
  • 2 tbsp peanut flour (optional)
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt

Cooking instructions

Click a step to open or close it.

Step 1: Prepare the vegetables
  1. If the cassava leaves are frozen, let them thaw. Dice the eggplant and chop the onion and garlic.
Step 2: Simmer the leaves
  1. Put the cassava leaves, water, onion, garlic and eggplant in a pot and bring to a boil.
  2. Cover and simmer on low heat for 40 minutes, stirring from time to time.
Step 3: Finish and serve
  1. Add the spinach, peanut flour, palm oil and salt and cook for 10 more minutes.
  2. Serve hot with ugali, rice or plantains.

Where it comes from

Isombe is associated with Rwanda in East Africa.
