Chicken Tagine

North Africa · Morocco

About this dish

A tagine is both a dish and the cone-shaped clay pot it is cooked in. The pot traps steam, so the meat becomes very tender while it simmers slowly with preserved lemon, olives and spices such as ras el hanout, ginger and turmeric. It is a classic of Moroccan cooking and is usually served with bread or couscous.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken pieces (thighs or drumsticks)
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 preserved lemon, rind sliced
  • 0.5 cup green olives
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 0.25 cup fresh coriander, chopped

Cooking instructions

Click a step to open or close it.

Step 1: Season the chicken
  1. Mix the ras el hanout, ginger, turmeric, garlic and olive oil into a paste.
  2. Rub the paste over the chicken and leave it for at least 30 minutes.
Step 2: Start the stew
  1. Spread the onions at the bottom of a tagine or heavy pot and place the chicken on top.
  2. Pour in the water, cover, and bring to a gentle simmer.
Step 3: Slow cook and finish
  1. Cook on low heat for 45 minutes, until the chicken is tender.
  2. Add the preserved lemon and olives and cook for 15 more minutes.
  3. Sprinkle with fresh coriander and serve with bread or couscous.
