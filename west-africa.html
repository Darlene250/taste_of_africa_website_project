West Africa

Bold flavours, big pots and generous sharing.

Food culture

West African cooking is known for its bold, layered flavours. Cooks build rich sauces from tomatoes, onions and scotch bonnet peppers, then simmer them with rice, beans, yams or cassava. Food is a social event here: big pots are made for family, friends and celebrations.

Staples

  • Rice
  • Millet
  • Yam
  • Cassava
  • Plantain
  • Black-eyed peas
  • Groundnuts

Key flavours

  • Scotch bonnet
  • Ginger
  • Thyme
  • Locust beans

Cooking techniques

  • One-pot rice cooking, where the rice absorbs the sauce.
  • Slow-simmered stews and soups, often thickened with ground seeds or nuts.
  • Pounding yam or cassava into a smooth dough called fufu.
  • Grilling and smoking meat over open flames, such as suya skewers.
